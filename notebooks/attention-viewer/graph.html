How to read these results

These are descriptive associations, not causal effects. η² describes differences in means and can miss other distributional differences. AMI corrects for chance agreement; Cramér’s V and η² are unadjusted effect sizes. Small clusters can show unstable enrichment. No property is used to refit clusters.

Representation & projection details

Each point is one completed slide, mapped by organ and slide ID to the attention viewer. Finding branches and organs retain separate embedding spaces. Vectors are L2 normalized; directed kNN links use exact cosine distance with stable tie ordering. PCA, t-SNE, UMAP and grouped squares change positions, not neighbor identities. Grouped squares pack each group into a compact grid with small gaps; square areas reflect slide counts. Grouping is independent of coloring. Positions within and between squares do not encode similarity.

t-SNE uses up to 50 PCA components, PCA initialization and a fixed seed. Distances between distant t-SNE clusters do not measure slide similarity. UMAP uses cosine distance on normalized embeddings, PCA initialization and a fixed seed. Its neighbor count and minimum distance are set in the viewer configuration. Distances between distant UMAP clusters do not measure slide similarity. These supervised representations include training slides.
